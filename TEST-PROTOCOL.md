# TEST-PROTOCOL.md
## Controlled Comparison Protocol for Ring of 12 v2
## Date: 6 November 2026

### Purpose
Demonstrate improvement (or lack thereof) before promoting the revised procedural directives to production. Do not declare improvement; measure it.

### Test set
1. Original study question: “How can I get better at not letting people use me”
2. Second test question: “Now that I am 60 years old and a gay man how can I avoid being used and still get a date with a possible relationship or even a NSA one?”
3. At least three additional domain-diverse questions (scientific/analytic, historical, purely practical non-boundary, etc.) to be supplied by the architect.

### Conditions
- Baseline: current production prompts (pre-v2).
- Revised: PROMPTS-v2.md directives applied to seats and Agent Zero.

### Scoring dimensions (score anonymized outputs where feasible)
- Factual grounding / unsupported assumptions
- Preservation of meaningful dissent
- Actionability
- User’s ability to distinguish evidence from interpretation
- Clarity of dual metrics (coincidence vs. verification)
- Appropriate use of reclaimed-capacity and alternative-check (conditional/mandatory rules followed)

### Method
- Run each question under both conditions.
- Prefer independent reviewers who do not know which version produced which output.
- Record full inputs, outputs, model version, and prompt version together.
- Append results to the chronological study document rather than overwriting prior conclusions.

### Promotion gate
Promote v2 to production only if comparative results show net improvement on the scoring dimensions without loss of the multi-lens observation character that defines the Ring.

### Versioning
All prompts, directives, scoring definitions, and outputs versioned together (e.g., PROMPTS-v2 + TEST-PROTOCOL-v1 + dated run artifacts).
