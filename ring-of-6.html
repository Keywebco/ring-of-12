Six Lenses, One Ring

The Ring of 6 is a six-lens inquiry ring, sitting alongside the Ring of 12. Each lens carries a different bias, reflecting the opinion of its god. Names are irrelevant, roles are always the same.

Ring
of 6 1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. Mind — Zeus, authority and knowledge.
    Sampling Bias. Failing to randomize your participants so your findings are skewed.
  2. Heart — opposite of Zeus, overly emotional / excess / feeling.
    Acquiescence Bias. The tendency to agree out of politeness or laziness rather than giving an honest view.
  3. History — no god named yet.
    Question Order Bias. The sequence of your questions influences how people answer later ones.
  4. Hands — no god named yet.
    Interviewer Bias. The tone, expressions, or background of the person asking unintentionally guides the respondent’s answers.
  5. Eyes — no god named yet.
    Non-Response Bias. People who don’t respond to a survey are fundamentally different from those who do.
  6. Truth — no god named yet.
    Social Desirability Bias. Answering in a way that makes you look good or socially acceptable rather than telling the truth.

The six lenses and their biases, in Roger’s order, approved by Roger 2026-11-11. Filed in full at ring6/LENSES.md in the offspring-seed repo.

The Principle

Each lens carries a different bias, reflecting the opinion / skew of its god. Names are irrelevant, roles are always the same — different ways to describe it. Put all together, variation and opinions cause the facts to come out and the debate to be won or lost. The combined biased views give an opinion; the debate among them is what brings the facts out.

This is the same pattern as the Ring of Twelve, viewed from a different position, with different names — Roger’s words, 2026-11-11: “Everything is identical to what we have. It’s just different names for the same thing.”

Six Stations, One Loop

The stations are not the lenses. A station is a seat in the standing loop. Every task passes around the loop. No permanent chair. Circular. Human and AI partners sit at every station — no named minds at any station.

The loop runs: Plus → Minus → Null → Past → Present → Future → back to Plus.

  1. Plus. What is added, what grows, what is gained.
  2. Minus. What is removed, what shrinks, what is lost.
  3. Null. Neither, zero, empty, no change, no value.
  4. Past. What was, what happened before, what is already done.
  5. Present. What is, what is happening now, what is true in this moment.
  6. Future. What will be, what is coming, what is planned, what could be.

Station names and purposes are Roger’s words, 2026-11-11. The detailed station contracts (checks, record, authority, handoff) are proposed, not canon, and are not shown here as Roger’s words. Full file: ring6/STATIONS.md.
